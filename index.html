Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how our WhatsApp assistant ("the assistant"), operated by Gift Mazinda in Nairobi, Kenya, handles your information.

What we collect

When you message the assistant on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your name as shown on WhatsApp, and the content of your messages. If you make a booking, we also record the name, service and time you give us.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your questions, make and manage bookings, and alert the business owner when you need help from a person. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Messages are delivered through WhatsApp (Meta) and replies are written with the help of an AI service provided by Anthropic. These providers process your messages to deliver the service.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for as long as needed to run the service. Chat history held by the assistant is temporary and is cleared when the assistant restarts.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Please do not send passwords, M-Pesa PINs or card details in chat. The assistant will never ask for them.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: giftmazinda@gmail.com
